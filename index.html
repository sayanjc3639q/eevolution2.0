B.Tech (Honours) MOOCs Requirements

Total Credits Required

4-Year B.Tech: 20 Credits total

Lateral Entry: 12 Credits total

Required: 4-8 credits/year (4 in Final Year)

1st Year Mandatory Skills

Must complete courses in at least 3 skills:

  • Programming (Python/R)
  • Soft Skills
  • Values & Ethics

Strict Credit Rules

CANNOT be double-counted for standard MAR points.

CANNOT be from the standard offline curriculum.

Mandatory for the Honours degree.

About EEvolution 2.0

EEvolution 2.0 is the definitive digital terminal for the Batch 2 Electrical Engineering community. Born from the need for a unified platform, it centralizes academic resources, real-time campus updates, and peer-to-peer memories into one high-performance interface.

  • High-speed document access
  • Exclusive Batch Community feed
  • Gamified rewards with Evo-Coins
